When we call collection.add(),  ChromaDB doesn't simply append everything to an array.  
ChromaDB updates or builds its internal vector index.

it organizes vectors into a structure,  optimized for nearest-neighbor search using 
alogrithms (HNSW or ANN implementation) - Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) indexing.
The vectors are organized so ChromaDB can quickly find nearby vectors without checking 
every single one. ChromaDB searches across all indexed vectors.
